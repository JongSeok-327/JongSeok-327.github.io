# Kongi Publisher Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Kongi Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by JONGSEOK BAE ("the developer") for publishing the developer's own short videos. The only Google account that signs in to the app is the developer's own account for the YouTube channel [@kongi_office](https://www.youtube.com/@kongi_office).

## 1. Google user data the app accesses

With the developer's consent through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube API Services to:

- upload videos and set their title, description, translations, playlist, publish time and AI disclosure (`youtube.upload`, `youtube`);
- read the developer's own channel information to confirm the correct channel is connected (`youtube`);
- read YouTube Analytics reports for the developer's own videos (`yt-analytics.readonly`).

## 2. How the data is used and stored

- The data is used only to publish the developer's videos and to review their performance.
- OAuth tokens and analytics numbers are stored only on the developer's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the developer.
- The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
- The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 3. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time at [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or through the [Google security settings page](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions); the developer then deletes the stored tokens and analytics data from the computer.

## 4. YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). See also the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Contact

Questions about this policy: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/JongSeok-327/JongSeok-327.github.io/issues)

## 6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and its effective date are posted on this page.
