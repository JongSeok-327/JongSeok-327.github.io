# Kongi Publisher

**Kongi Publisher** is a private publishing tool used only by the creator of the short-video series 「コンイ | 会社員ねこ」 (YouTube [@kongi_office](https://www.youtube.com/@kongi_office)).
It runs on the creator's own computer and is not offered to other users.

What it does, after the creator approves each episode:

- uploads the finished short video to the creator's own YouTube channel, with the title, description, translated titles (ko/en), playlist and the "altered or synthetic content" (AI) disclosure;
- schedules the publish time;
- reads YouTube Analytics for the creator's own videos (views, watch time, engaged views) to decide future episodes.

It does not access any other person's account or data.
